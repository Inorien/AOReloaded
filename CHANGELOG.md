# Changelog

What changed in each AOReloaded release, newest first.

## Unreleased

## 0.3.3 - 2026-10-29

### Fixed
- On the classic 18.8.62 client (AO-Andersson, AORebirth's classic client) the cast bars, autorun, mouse-run, the
  number pad in the chat, Ctrl+click and Alt+click in the inventory, pinned items and the skills window's Favorites
  were switched off: they only knew Project Rubi-Ka's client. They now work on both.

## 0.3.1 - 2026-10-27

### Changed
- AOReloaded's features now come as `aoreloaded.dll`, an AOPlugins plugin, with their tab in the options window.

### New
- Inventory: Ctrl+click moves an item into the last opened backpack (and from a backpack back to the inventory);
  Alt+click selects several items to move together; Ctrl+Alt+click pins items and stacks, marked with "* ".
- Skills window: a Favorites group (hidden rows collapsed), and Shift+click on +/- raises or lowers by 5.

### Fixed
- A crash when closing a container.
